## Scope

index.html, the whole one-page site. Visitor mode: Persuade (the visitor decides Dorna is credible and gets in touch).

## Audience, job, proof

Hiring managers, founders, press/organizers arriving from LinkedIn, a resume, or an intro; they need to confirm seniority and range fast. Proof is the real record only: bio, 6 publications, 3 articles, 7 press items, 7 previous orgs, 4 features with photos, education. Primary action: email dpourang@mindrisemedica.com.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A senior executive's personal site, played straight: name, role, portrait and an email action in the first viewport, then the record laid out as a calm, scannable dossier. It refuses the biotech-corporate template (stock lab imagery, molecule graphics, metric tiles) and the psychedelic-wellness template (gradients, trippy color).

OWN-WORLD: Near-white cool paper ground, deep navy-charcoal ink, one deep petrol accent for links and the action. Source Serif 4 for the name and section titles, Hanken Grotesk for everything else, self-hosted. Hairline rules, no cards except the four feature photos. Lists with a tabular year/outlet column.

STORY: Visitor learns who she is (clinical ops and strategy, oncology and next-gen therapeutics, first-in-human through approval), sees the second track (psychedelic medicine access, VA program), scans publications, writing, press and orgs, and emails her.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim sticky header (name left, section links right). Left two-thirds: "Hi, I'm Dorna Pourang." at display scale in the serif, a one-line role statement, the first bio paragraph, then a filled "Email me" button and a quiet "View publications" link. Right third: the portrait, tall crop, square corners. Previous-orgs row sits at the bottom edge of the viewport.

FORM: canon (classic executive site), user's choice over the dealt direction. seed:3f4428b8935f7bf7

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
