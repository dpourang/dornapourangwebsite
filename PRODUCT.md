## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: plain static HTML/CSS (single index.html, no build step). Chosen because the site is a one-page personal site deployed on Vercel from GitHub with push-to-deploy, and Dorna edits it through Claude; zero build config means zero build failures.

## Users

People evaluating Dorna Pourang professionally: biotech/pharma hiring managers and founders weighing her for clinical operations work, plus press, podcast hosts, and event organizers in the psychedelic-medicine space. Dorna stated no single priority audience, so the page serves them in balance. They arrive from a LinkedIn profile, a resume, an introduction, or a podcast/article and want to confirm credibility fast.

## Product Purpose

A personal site that makes Dorna's credibility legible in one visit: who she is, the depth of her clinical development record, her published work, her writing and press, and how to reach her. Success: a visitor leaves convinced she is a serious senior operator and knows how to contact her.

## Positioning

Two tracks most people in clinical operations do not hold together: nearly two decades running oncology and next-generation therapeutic programs from first-in-human through approval, and a public role in psychedelic-medicine access (including helping establish the first psychedelic-assisted therapy program for veterans with PTSD in the VA system).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Dorna Pourang. First-person voice ("I'm a clinical trial operations and strategy specialist...") from the existing bio.
- Standing direction (chosen 2026-10-15 over two bolder concepts): the classic senior-executive personal site, played straight and executed at the craft level of the best executive/board-member sites. Convention is the commitment; no novelty concept.
- Redesign: the current black/white Squarespace template look (Ambroise serif, Proxima Nova, DIN Condensed, all caps) is evidence, not a constraint; Dorna explicitly does not want it copied.

## Product Principles

1. Credibility first: the record speaks; design gets out of its way and makes it scannable.
2. Human, not corporate: warmth and a point of view, not a biotech template.
3. Every claim is real and linked where a source exists.
4. Fast and durable: static, self-hosted assets, nothing that breaks when a vendor changes.
