Hi, I’m Dorna Pourang.

Clinical trial operations and strategy Oncology and next-generation therapeutics

I’m a clinical trial operations and strategy specialist working at the forefront of innovative oncology and advanced next-generation therapeutics. For nearly two decades, I’ve driven multiple therapeutics from first-in-human studies through clinical development and into approval, leading early-phase strategy and results-driven execution across biotech and pharma. My work spans complex biologics, emerging modalities, and global clinical infrastructure, with a focus on building scalable, ethical, and patient-centered trial systems.

Alongside my industry work, I’m passionate about expanding access to evidence-based mental health treatments. This includes helping establish the first psychedelic-assisted therapy program for veterans with PTSD within the VA hospital system and consulting across early regulatory and health-equity frameworks in the field. Above all, I’m committed to innovative, patient-centered care models that ensure breakthrough science reaches the patients who need it most.
